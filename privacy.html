Ghat Racers Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Ghat Racers is a racing game made by Rinshad Nadukkandi (an individual developer). This page explains what the game does with your information. In short: there are no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and the game does not sell or share your data for advertising.

What stays on your phone

Your best scores, best-run ghosts, chosen ride, paint colours, traffic level, the name you pick and whether you bought the full game are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Racing with friends

  • Nearby race (same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot) connects phones directly on your local network. Nothing goes over the internet. On iPhone the game asks for Local Network permission for this.
  • Online race uses a connection service (PeerJS) only to introduce the phones to each other. After that the race data travels directly between the players' phones. The service sees your IP address while connecting, as any internet service does.
  • The name you choose and your ride's colours are shown to the other players in your race. They are not stored on any server. Names are checked for bad words, phone numbers and links before they are shown.

Buying the full game

Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; the game never sees your card or bank details. To know whether you own the full game, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt from the store. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Google privacy policy and the Apple privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. There is no chat; players only see each other's chosen names and a few preset quick messages.

Your choices

You can play alone without ever using a race feature. You can delete everything stored on your phone by deleting the app.

Deleting your data

Ghat Racers has no accounts. The only data kept off your phone is the anonymous purchase record RevenueCat keeps so you can restore the full game.

  1. Email devrinshad1@gmail.com with the subject "Ghat Racers data deletion".
  2. Say which store you bought from (Google Play or App Store) and, if you can, the order number from your purchase receipt.
  3. The anonymous purchase record is deleted from RevenueCat within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done.

What is kept: the store itself (Google or Apple) keeps its own record of your payment as required by law; that is covered by their privacy policies. Your unlock is not lost: the store still knows you bought it, so tapping "Restore purchase" later brings it back and creates a new anonymous record.
